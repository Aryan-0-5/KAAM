Kaam privacy policy

Last updated 31 October 2026

Kaam is a private task tracker used by one family. It is not offered to the public. Only family members given a PIN by the family's admin can sign in.

What Kaam stores

  • Tasks, updates, notes and lists of things needed, as typed in by family members.
  • Each family member's name, a scrambled (hashed) PIN, and optionally their Gmail address.
  • Documents and photos that family members attach to tasks.

Google Drive

The family's admin connects one Google account. Kaam uses Google's drive.file permission, which only lets it see and manage files and folders that Kaam itself created. It cannot see anything else in that Google Drive.

Kaam creates a folder called "Kaam" in the admin's Drive, saves attached documents there, renames and moves them to keep them organised, and shares that folder (view only) with the Gmail addresses the admin enters for family members. The admin's email address is read once, to show which account is connected.

Where data is kept

Task details are stored in a private database used only by this app. Documents are stored only in the admin's Google Drive. Nothing is sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any purpose other than running Kaam for this family.

Removing data

The admin can disconnect Google Drive at any time from Kaam's settings, or remove Kaam's access from their Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Family members can ask the admin to delete their tasks or remove them from the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy go to the family's admin, who manages this app.
